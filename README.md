# VibeStudio-AI
Instead of creating another generic AI image generator, build a product that turns one product photo and a short business brief into a complete, editable advertising campaign—with consistent branding, a storyboard, generated visuals, voice-over, and ready-to-publish social content.
# Problem:
Small businesses, independent sellers, and local brands often need several kinds of advertising content for every product launch. Hiring a photographer, designer, copywriter, and video editor for each campaign can be expensive and slow.
Generic AI tools can generate attractive individual assets, but users still have to coordinate the story, keep the brand consistent, and assemble the final campaign.
# Target user: A small e-commerce brand or marketing freelancer who needs a campaign for a product but lacks a dedicated creative team.
# Core promise: From one product photo to a coherent campaign draft in minutes, with less manual editing.
